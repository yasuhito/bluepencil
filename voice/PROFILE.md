# Company voice profile

## Who we are
- Company / product: Qni — a quantum circuit simulator and tools for developers
- Audience: developers
- Channels we write most: changelog, email, GitHub
- Spelling: American English
- Timezone: Asia/Tokyo

## Rules
<!-- Format: - **Rule.** "quoted example from a sample the human approved" (sample NNN) -->
- **Lead with the user-visible result, not the work behind it.** "Faster test runs, and a fix for the flaky retry" (sample 001)
- **Give the real number, before and after.** "We cut median test time from 6m40s to 2m10s" (sample 001)
- **Say "we" and own what we did, including the mistakes.** "We also fixed the retry bug" (sample 001)
- **Apologize in one word when we broke something. No excuses, no explanation of why it happened.** "If you saw green builds you didn't trust last week, that was this. Sorry." (sample 001)
- **Describe a bug from the reader's seat: what they saw, not what the code did.** "a test that failed once would sometimes be marked as passed on the second run without actually re-running" (sample 001)
- **Say what the reader has to do. If nothing, say that.** "Nothing to configure; it's on for everyone." (sample 001)
- **Small changes get one sentence each, grouped and labeled as small.** "Two smaller things: the log viewer now wraps long lines, and you can copy a single line with one click." (sample 001)
- **Close with a real reply path and a human behind it.** "Questions? Reply to this email and a human will answer." (sample 001)
- **Contractions and plain words. No adjectives that sell.** "it's on for everyone" (not "seamlessly enabled for all users") (sample 001)
- **Say what it does, not what it is.** "running suites in parallel across four workers" (not "a powerful parallel CI solution") (sample 001)

## Words we avoid
- Marketing adjectives: powerful, seamless, robust, blazing, next-generation
- "Users" when we mean "you"
- Exclamation marks
- Ticket ids in outbound text

## Words we use on purpose
- "we" (the company), "you" (the reader)
- "Sorry." as a full sentence
- Exact times and counts (6m40s, four workers)

## Default edit depth
- `light` | `medium` | `deep` → `medium`
