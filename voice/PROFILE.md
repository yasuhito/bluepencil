## Default edit depth

`light` | `medium` | `deep` → `medium`

- `light` — typos, grammar, obvious filler. Structure untouched.
- `medium` — the above, plus sentence order, cuts, and channel shape.
- `deep` — rebuild it. Only the facts survive.
